Rewrite CV content clearly, truthfully, and professionally.

Goal:
Create concise, high-impact resume bullets that balance:

technical depth,
business impact,
leadership potential,
stakeholder communication,
and operational ownership.

The CV should position the candidate as a versatile senior data scientist capable of:

building production-grade systems,
communicating with technical and non-technical stakeholders,
supporting strategic decisions,
mentoring collaborators,
and adapting across domains and projects.

General Style:

Around 15-20 words per bullet.
Write in a confident, senior-level tone.
Prioritize clarity and readability over buzzwords.
Keep wording natural and human.
Avoid generic corporate language.
Avoid keyword stuffing.
Preserve factual meaning.

Core Emphasis:

Prioritize measurable business and financial impact as the primary narrative of the role.
Emphasize end-to-end ownership of projects.
Emphasize collaboration with stakeholders, leadership teams, and cross-functional partners.
Highlight communication, coordination, and decision-support responsibilities.
Highlight mentorship, technical guidance, and ad hoc leadership when present.
Emphasize scientific rigor, reproducibility, validation, and analytical thinking.
Show adaptability across research, engineering, analytics, and business contexts.
Explicitly include dollar-value savings, revenue impact, operational gains, and performance metrics whenever available.

Metric Prioritization:

Always prioritize financial and business impact metrics over technical metrics whenever both are available.
Prefer:
dollar savings,
revenue impact,
operational efficiency gains,
cost reductions,
processing time reductions,
resource optimization,
business scalability improvements.
Technical metrics (MAPE, AUC, precision, latency, etc.) should support the business narrative, not replace it.
When possible, combine business and technical impact in the same bullet.
If a role contains measurable financial impact, ensure at least one bullet explicitly communicates it.
Never omit dollar-value impact metrics when available.
Never include information of reducing head count, staff size or human resources.

Role-Level Impact Enforcement:

If a role contains explicit financial impact metrics, prioritize those metrics in the first or second bullet of that role.
Financial impact metrics must take precedence over implementation details.
For technically dense roles, summarize tooling more aggressively in order to preserve space for business outcomes.
Do not allow cloud infrastructure, orchestration, or modeling details to overshadow measurable business value.
When both are available, structure bullets to communicate:
business impact,
operational outcome,
technical implementation.
Prefer business-first phrasing whenever possible.

Technical Writing Guidance:

Prefer describing systems, workflows, and outcomes instead of isolated tools.
Mention technologies naturally within accomplishments.
Emphasize productionization, scalability, automation, orchestration, optimization, and reliability where applicable.
Highlight cloud, MLOps, distributed workflows, or deployment experience when relevant.
Preserve important technical terminology.
Compress excessive implementation detail when stronger business impact information is available.
Use easy to understand values (dollars, percentage, etc...)

Leadership & Collaboration Guidance:

Highlight influence without exaggerating formal authority.
Emphasize cross-functional collaboration, stakeholder alignment, and strategic support responsibilities.
Present mentorship and guidance naturally when supported by the source material.
Reflect flexibility in handling ad hoc projects, operational demands, and evolving business priorities.
Emphasize communication with senior leadership and decision-makers whenever applicable.

Strong Bullet Characteristics:

Action-oriented.
Outcome-driven.
Technically credible.
Easy to scan quickly.
Reflect seniority without exaggeration.

Rules:

Do not invent experience, responsibilities, metrics, tools, leadership, or results.
Do not fabricate management experience.
Do not overstate impact.
Do not remove relevant metrics.
Do not omit stakeholder interaction if present.
Return plain text only.
The forecasting reduction in execution time was at carrefour, not Ria.
