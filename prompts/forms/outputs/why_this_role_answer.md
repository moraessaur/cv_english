At Ria Money Transfer, I led the development of an end-to-end forecasting pipeline to predict transaction volumes per country and agent up to seven days ahead. This project aimed to automate and improve the accuracy of existing manual forecasting processes, directly impacting financial KPIs such as reducing surplus capital and lockout rates. For example, in Mexico, the model contributed to a daily reduction of approximately $500,000 in underfunding and a 35% decrease in lockout rates. I presented the initial proof of concept to the CFO, secured project approval, and maintained regular stakeholder communication throughout. Technically, I designed a scalable, parameterized Python package using pandas and scikit-learn, deployed via AWS services including S3, Athena, Glue, and ECR with CI/CD pipelines integrated through GitHub Actions and Terraform. Model performance was continuously monitored using metrics like adjusted MAPE and MAE, and seasonal event modeling further enhanced accuracy.

Additionally, at Melhor Envio, I applied data science to financial customer behavior by developing a real-time pipeline to monitor client activity patterns and churn risk. This work informed retention strategies that recovered over 70 clients, increased response rates to retention calls by 20%, and improved coupon conversion rates by 30%. I created a client segmentation model using the K-prototype algorithm that enabled targeted marketing efforts, optimizing coupon distribution based on customer profiles and increasing their utilization. I also built a logistic regression churn prediction model incorporating features derived from prior analyses, achieving an AUC of around 0.75. My analysis and dashboards, developed primarily in R and Looker, supported data-driven decisions impacting customer lifetime value and company revenue.

These experiences demonstrate my ability to apply data science rigorously to finance-related problems, combining statistical modeling, programming, and stakeholder engagement to deliver measurable business impact.
