About the job
Who we are

With 8 years of history, TerraMagna is driven by the mission of making agriculture everything the world needs it to be. Today, it operates as a financier of the agribusiness input supply chain, aiming to become its operating system and main financial partner.

The company manages over BRL 1 billion in assets, has raised BRL 75 million in funding, and serves clients across Brazil.

The Challenge and the Modeling Team

As a startup in constant development, our environment is dynamic and requires autonomy. The Modeling Team is lean and focused on solving complex problems related to credit risk and data analysis in agribusiness.

We are looking for an experienced Data Scientist with a profile oriented toward mentorship and teamwork.

We believe in a free and flexible work environment, where trust is the foundation of everything we do. We value people who are driven by new challenges, capable of exploring the agribusiness sector, and helping us deliver its most important input: credit.

Day-to-day
Develop, implement, and monitor statistical and machine learning models, especially credit scoring and risk analysis models.
Productionize models, working autonomously from conception to deployment in production environments.
Collaborate with technical leadership in defining data architecture and the team’s technology choices.
Translate complex business problems into data-driven solutions, clearly communicating results to both technical and non-technical audiences.
Collaborate dynamically with product and engineering teams.
Stay up to date with trends and best practices in Data Science and Engineering.
Requirements
3+ years of experience as a Data Scientist, preferably in startups or tech companies.
Strong foundation in probability, statistics, and both supervised and unsupervised machine learning models.
Proficiency in Python and key data science libraries (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, etc.).
Strong SQL skills (data modeling, performance optimization, and handling large datasets).
Experience developing and operationalizing end-to-end machine learning pipelines in production (data exploration and preparation, cleaning, feature engineering, modeling, and evaluation), with focus on scalability, reproducibility, security, and continuous performance monitoring.
Experience with engineering practices for model production, including version control (Git/GitHub), cloud deployment, workflow automation, and supporting monitoring and maintenance of production models.
Familiarity with cloud services, preferably Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
Proactive profile, ability to work on ambiguous problems, autonomy to structure analytical approaches, and excellent communication skills for alignment with multiple stakeholders.
Genuine interest in technical growth within a dynamic fintech environment.
