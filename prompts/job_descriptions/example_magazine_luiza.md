We offer solutions such as:

Digital accounts
Receivables management

Acquiring, credit cards, lending, Pix, and much more

Our purpose is clear: to digitalize businesses and promote financial inclusion, especially among Brazilian entrepreneurs.

At Magalupay, data and Machine Learning are the backbone of our strategy. We are looking for a Principal Data Scientist to act as a senior technical leader, working directly with our Business Units to design and implement solutions that drive our credit decision-making.

Role Description

As a technical expert, you will oversee the full lifecycle of high-criticality models, ensuring they support accurate and high-impact business decisions. Your mission is to extract maximum value from these models to make our credit strategy the most competitive, fair, and secure in the market.

What You Will Do
Technical Leadership: Lead the development, validation, and monitoring of ML models with direct business impact.
Strategic Partnership: Translate complex business challenges into actionable Data Science solutions for Credit teams.
Performance Optimization: Conduct robust risk analyses to identify improvement opportunities in production models.
Architecture & Scalability: Influence the design of data and ML architecture, ensuring technological robustness.
Stakeholder Management: Communicate technical results and strategic recommendations clearly to leadership.
Autonomy & Big Picture Thinking: Define technical priorities with full focus on overall organizational outcomes.
What We’re Looking For
Senior/Principal Experience: Proven track record developing ML models for critical decision-making processes (preferably in Finance/Credit).
Technical Expertise: Strong knowledge of Python, SQL, and machine learning tools and algorithms.
Solid understanding of classical statistics, hypothesis testing, and experimentation (e.g., causal inference).
System Design & Operationalization: Ability to design scalable and robust ML solutions, contributing to architecture decisions (feature stores, model registries, automated pipelines) alongside engineering teams. Candidates should understand the full model lifecycle—from experimentation to deployment—and be able to reduce friction between development and production, ensuring consistent and autonomous value delivery.
Standardization & Governance: Experience building abstraction layers and internal libraries that enforce best practices and standardize the ML lifecycle. This ensures technical consistency, traceability (data/model lineage), and regulatory compliance, enabling business teams to innovate within safe technical guardrails.
Engineering Culture: Hands-on experience with Git/GitHub in production environments.
Problem Solving: Strong analytical skills to diagnose risks and propose data-driven solutions.
Communication & Influence: Ability to present complex analyses to both technical and executive audiences.
Languages: Advanced English (reading and speaking).
Nice to Have
Experience with MLOps and modern data warehousing tools (SageMaker, Vertex AI, MLflow, Athena, Redshift, BigQuery, Snowflake, etc.).
ML at Scale Architecture: Experience designing cloud-based ML architectures that support multiple models in production simultaneously, with a strong understanding of trade-offs (batch vs. online, real-time inference, cost vs. latency, etc.).
Building from Scratch: Experience structuring ML teams or functions from the ground up, defining infrastructure, development processes, validation, and monitoring to enable scalable growth.
Postgraduate degree (Master’s or PhD) in quantitative fields.
