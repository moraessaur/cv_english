Job Description

We are looking for a Senior Data Scientist to work with one of our strategic clients in the financial services / payments sector, contributing to the development of analytical models and Machine Learning solutions that support strategic and operational decision-making.

We are seeking a technical, analytical professional with strong business acumen, capable of working end to end, from data exploration and modeling to communicating results to different stakeholders.

Responsibilities

Develop, validate, and improve statistical and Machine Learning models, ensuring performance, stability, and explainability
Conduct end-to-end analyses: exploration, preparation, modeling, and delivery
Apply predictive modeling, clustering, anomaly detection, and forecasting techniques to business problems
Work with large volumes of data, ensuring quality, integrity, and statistical consistency
Contribute to strategic use cases by translating business needs into data-driven analytical solutions
Support model deployment in collaboration with Data Engineering and MLOps teams
Communicate results and insights clearly to both technical and business audiences
Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams, including engineering, analytics, product, and business teams
Requirements and Qualifications

Experience in predictive modeling, machine learning, and statistical analysis
Proficiency in Python and/or R and ML libraries such as scikit-learn, TensorFlow, etc.
Experience with clustering, anomaly detection, and forecasting techniques
Preferably, knowledge of the Databricks platform for ML deployment/exposure
Ability to translate analytical results into business insights
Strong SQL knowledge for manipulating and analyzing large volumes of data
Experience with complementary data science libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, XGBoost, PyTorch, or similar
Knowledge of Feature Engineering and, preferably, Feature Store
Experience with PySpark and distributed data processing
Familiarity with cloud environments, preferably Azure, including Azure ML, ADLS, and Data Factory
Knowledge of MLOps practices, including model versioning, deployment, and monitoring, with experience in MLflow or similar tools
Ability to conduct analyses autonomously, with critical thinking and business vision
Clear and accessible communication skills with different audiences
Nice to Have

Experience with transactional data and/or fraud prevention
Experience in regulated or high-criticality environments
Knowledge of visualization tools such as Power BI, Tableau, or similar
Postgraduate degree, master’s, or PhD in quantitative fields such as Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Economics, or related areas
Additional Information

Competitive compensation based on experience
Opportunity for growth within the company and participation in strategic projects
Dynamic and challenging work environment
Opportunity to work at a company in a rapidly expanding market
