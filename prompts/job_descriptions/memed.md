Memed is an innovative healthtech transforming the relationship between doctors, patients, and the pharmaceutical industry through technology. Our purpose is to simplify access to medical information and improve the quality of healthcare in Brazil.

With digital solutions that impact millions of lives every day, we are building a more efficient, connected, and data-driven ecosystem, shaping the future of healthcare in the country.

The impact of your work

As a Senior Data Scientist, you will play a strategic role in analyzing, interpreting, and transforming data into insights that guide business, product, and technology decisions.

Your work will be essential for developing statistical and Machine Learning models, supporting the evolution of Memed’s products, increasing operational efficiency, and driving initiatives that impact doctors, patients, and partners within our ecosystem.

You will work closely with multidisciplinary squads, contributing to hypothesis formulation, experiment execution, result validation, and clear communication of analyses to different audiences across the organization.

Responsibilities and assignments

Understand business needs and translate them into analytical demands, hypotheses, technical requirements, and execution plans.
Develop and implement advanced Machine Learning models, predictive and prescriptive analyses applied to activation, engagement, and retention challenges.
Structure statistical methodologies for behavior analysis, segmentation, cohorts, and tracking key metrics, ensuring consistency, accuracy, and reproducibility.
Evaluate the impact of campaigns and business interventions using causal inference methodologies and econometric models, including Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM), uplift modeling, and quasi-experimental approaches (Diff-in-Diffs, Synthetic Control, Propensity Score Matching).
Develop statistical frameworks for incrementality measurement, channel attribution, and marketing investment optimization, connecting analytical insights to strategic product, growth, and partnership decisions.
Interpret and communicate complex information about doctors, patients, and partners, generating strategic insights for product, technology, marketing, and corporate teams.
Collaborate closely with Product, Engineering, Marketing, and Operations teams, turning data into practical and actionable recommendations.
Ensure best practices for documentation, versioning, governance, and data quality.
Act as a technical reference, supporting solution reviews, analytical standards definition, and dissemination of Data Science best practices.
