Senior Data Scientist – Mindera
About the Role

At Mindera, we believe that software is built by people, for people. We create high-performance systems that impact users worldwide.

We are looking for a Senior Data Scientist to join an agile, collaborative team where your voice matters as much as your code.

You won’t just be building technical solutions—you’ll be a strategic partner helping us create resilient systems that scale.

We value empathy, self-organization, collaboration, and the courage to take risks. If you enjoy solving complex problems and believe teaching others is one of the best ways to learn, you'll feel right at home.

You will work closely with company leadership (CEO/COO) and clients, so strong communication skills and a collaborative mindset are essential.

Expected travel: 0–15%, depending on project, client, and organizational needs.

Responsibilities
Business Insight & Problem Definition
Partner closely with Marketing and Product teams to diagnose business challenges.
Define metrics, analyses, and interpretations that support strategic decision-making.
Statistical Modeling
Define requirements for, develop, monitor, and continuously improve statistical models.
Focus on marketing spend optimization and product growth initiatives.
Narrative & Visualization
Create clear narratives and visualizations to communicate insights and model outputs to non-technical audiences.
Rapid Prototyping
Independently prototype and validate new ideas, moving from hypothesis to rigorous testing with minimal supervision.
Technical Consultation
Evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of different modeling approaches.
Select and iterate on solutions appropriate for specific marketing and growth problems.
