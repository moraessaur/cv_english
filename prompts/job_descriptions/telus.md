This role is fully remote for candidates based in Brazil. If you are based in São Paulo or Porto Alegre, you are welcome to work from one of our offices on a flexible schedule.

The Opportunity

As a Data Scientist / Machine Learning Engineer on our team, you'll design, develop, and deliver machine learning solutions, decipher loosely defined problems using complex datasets, and determine appropriate analytical and modeling techniques. You'll analyze structured and unstructured data, establish automated processes for large-scale modeling, perform exploratory analysis, and collaborate with peers on data acquisition and exploration, demonstrating a strong sense of ownership and urgency.

Responsibilities

    Work on all aspects of the design, development and delivery of machine learning enabled solutions for our clients;
    Come up with solutions to loosely defined business problems by leveraging pattern detection over complex datasets across multiple platforms;
    Establish efficient, automated processes for large-scale modeling;
    Become part of a highly skilled and geographically distributed team;
    Collaborate with peers on problem definitions, data acquisition, data exploration, visualization as well as building the tools that support this process;
    Strong sense of ownership, urgency and drive.

Qualifications

    3-5 years of professional experience in data science and machine learning development
    Bachelor's or Master's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science, or related field
    Proficiency in Python, SQL, and data manipulation libraries (pandas, NumPy); experience with ML frameworks (Keras, TensorFlow, PyTorch) and scikit-learn
    Strong understanding of machine learning algorithms and statistical methods, with proven ability to design, implement, and evaluate models end-to-end
    Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP), version control (Git), and model deployment practices
    Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with ability to translate technical findings to non-technical stakeholders
    Fluent in English (written and verbal)

Bonus Points

    Experience with containerization and ML deployment frameworks (Docker, Kubernetes, Kubeflow, MLFlow, or similar MLOps tools)
    Experience with big data technologies (Spark, Hadoop) or CI/CD pipelines for ML systems
