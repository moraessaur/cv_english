Scientist, Bioinformatics & Data Analytics

Position Purpose

We are seeking a skilled and motivated Scientist, Bioinformatics & Data Analytics, to support Microarray-related projects through advanced data analysis, workflow automation, and cross-functional scientific collaboration.

In this role, you will work closely with molecular biologists to analyze and interpret complex experimental data, generate actionable insights, and support data-driven decision-making. You will develop and apply bioinformatics and data analytics approaches to Microarray datasets, identify relationships between wet-lab processes and data outcomes, and contribute to Smart Factory and digitalization initiatives.

This is a hybrid position requiring 2 days per week on site; the remaining days may be worked remotely.

Key Responsibilities

    Collaborate with molecular biologists to understand experimental objectives and define data analysis strategies.
    Analyze complex Microarray datasets and derive clear, scientifically sound conclusions.
    Develop, implement, and maintain robust data analysis workflows to improve efficiency, scalability, and reproducibility.
    Automate data analysis processes to enable fast and reliable decision-making.
    Troubleshoot data- and assay-related issues, identify root causes, and implement corrective and preventive actions from a bioinformatics and data analytics perspective.
    Interpret results, generate hypotheses, and recommend next steps based on experimental findings.
    Plan, design, and execute bioinformatics and data analytics activities independently.
    Provide technical leadership in product and method development, optimization, and troubleshooting.
    Communicate analytical findings effectively to cross-functional stakeholders, including through technical and management-level presentations.
    Implement data analysis-related changes to existing assays, including test design, stakeholder alignment, and documentation updates.
    Ensure data quality, integrity, and appropriate quality control within the scope of responsibility.
    Collaborate effectively across teams, departments, and global functions.
    Continuously expand expertise in bioinformatics, data analytics, and relevant digital technologies.

Qualifications / Education / Experience

    MSc in Bioinformatics, Computational Biology, Data Science, or a related field, or equivalent relevant hands-on experience.
    Strong proficiency in Python and commonly used data analysis libraries.
    Demonstrated ability to write complex, modular, and maintainable code.
    Experience developing and maintaining data analysis workflows or pipelines independently.
    Familiarity with Linux and command-line environments.
    Experience with SQL and Git.
    Good understanding of core software development practices, including code organization, testing, and documentation.
    Strong ability to analyze, visualize, and interpret experimental data.
    Ability to work effectively with colleagues from diverse scientific and technical backgrounds.
    Strong presentation, communication, and technical writing skills.
    Strong written and spoken English communication skills.
    Strong attention to detail.

Preferred Qualifications

    Experience with Docker, AI/ML libraries, or web development technologies.
    Familiarity with AI-assisted coding tools and agentic workflows for software development and data analysis.
    Basic understanding of computer networking and PC hardware.
    Experience with Microarray data analysis or related genomics / molecular biology applications.
    Experience contributing to workflow automation, digitalization, or Smart Factory initiatives.
